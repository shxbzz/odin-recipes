Tacos

Ingredients

  • Small tortillas - 4
  • Minced beef - 250 grams
  • Cheddar cheese - 80 grams, grated
  • Lettuce - 50 grams, shredded
  • Tomato - 1, diced
  • Red onion - 1/4, diced
  • Taco seasoning - 1 tbsp
  • Salsa - 2 tbsp

Steps

  1. Heat a frying pan over medium heat
  2. Add the minced beef and cook until browned
  3. Add the taco seasoning and mix well
  4. Warm the tortillas in a separate pan
  5. Place some cooked beef into each tortilla
  6. Add lettuce, tomato, red onion and grated cheese
  7. Top with salsa and serve
