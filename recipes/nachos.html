Nachos

Ingredients

  • Tortilla chips - 150 grams
  • Cheddar cheese - 150 grams
  • Tomato - 1, diced
  • Jalapenos - 30 grams, sliced
  • Salsa - 3 tbsp
  • Sour cream - 2 tbsp

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C
  2. Spread the tortilla chips evenly on a baking tray
  3. Sprinkle the grated cheddar cheese over the tortilla chips
  4. Add the diced tomato and sliced jalapeños
  5. Bake for 5 to 10 minutes until the cheese has melted
  6. Serve with salsa and sour cream
