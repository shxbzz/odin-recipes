Quesadilla

Ingredients

  • Large flour tortillas - 2
  • Cheddar cheese - 120 grams, grated
  • Bell pepper - 1/2, sliced
  • Red onion - 1/4, sliced
  • Cooked chicken - 100 grams, chopped
  • Olive oil - 1 tsp

Steps

  1. Heat a frying pan over medium heat
  2. Place one tortilla in the pan
  3. Spread the grated cheese over the tortilla
  4. Add the chicken, bell pepper and red onion
  5. Place the second tortilla on top
  6. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes until the bottom is golden
  7. Flip the quesadilla and cook the other side for 2 to 3 minutes
  8. Remove from the pan, slice into pieces and serve
